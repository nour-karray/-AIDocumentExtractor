# Privacy

DocIA may process sensitive medical and financial documents. By default it stores structured extraction results but not original files or raw OCR text. Configure `DOCIA_STORE_SOURCE_FILES` and `DOCIA_STORE_RAW_TEXT` only after defining a lawful purpose, retention period, access policy, and deletion process.

Use synthetic data in tests and examples. Production operators remain responsible for consent, encryption, backups, access logging, data residency, and applicable regulations. DocIA is not a medical device and its output must be reviewed by a qualified person.
